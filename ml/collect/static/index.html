Thanks for helping build StrainlessAI

StrainlessAI listens to one short sung exercise and says whether it sounds healthy or strained. To teach it, we need many singers recording the same exercise.

  • About 20–30 minutes for 16 short takes of one arpeggio.
  • You can stop and come back any time with the same link. Every accepted take is saved.
  • You're paid $10 after all 16 takes are submitted.
  • You'll need a quiet room and your device's built-in mic, a wired headset or a USB mic. Not Bluetooth earbuds.

Mic check

One practice take, to check your room and mic before the real takes. It isn't saved.

  1. Sit or stand about an arm's length from the mic, and keep that distance for every take.
  2. Tap Record. You'll hear the starting note and 3 clicks.
  3. Wait while the screen says Wait…, then sing the arpeggio when it says Sing now.

Your 16 takes

Record the takes in order: comfortable first, pushed last, so the effortful takes can't tire your voice before the easy ones. The notes never change; only the effort does.
